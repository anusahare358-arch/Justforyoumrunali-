JUST FOR YOU - WEBSITE

Extract this ZIP and open index.html to preview.
For a shareable link, upload the extracted folder to a static website host.
Keep index.html, style.css, script.js and the assets folder together.

All 9 photographs and the complete Enna Sona audio are included.
Click Show me to begin the photo story and music.
Music stays playing while you change photographs.
The Enna Sona button can pause and resume the music.
The final smile buttons show a local response; they do not send a message.

To personalise the text, edit index.html and the photo captions in script.js.
